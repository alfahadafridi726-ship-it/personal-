# Happy National Princess Day - Princess Hafiza Edition 👑💖

A royal, interactive web application created for **National Princess Day (November 18)** dedicated to **Hafiza**.

---

## 🌟 The Magical Journey for Princess Hafiza

When your princess scans the royal QR Code or opens the link, she experiences this heartfelt sequence:

1. **👑 Step 1: Royal Greeting & Happy Princess Day**
   - Floating 3D golden tiara with sparkle effects, rose petals, and ambient fairy-tale melody chimes.
   - Grand royal dedication celebrating Hafiza as the only royalty in your kingdom.
2. **💍 Step 2: The Heartfelt Royal Proposal**
   - 3D diamond ring box with glowing halo.
   - The question: *"Hafiza, Will You Be My Forever Princess & Queen? 💍💖"*
   - **Interactive "YES" Button**: Triggers a celebratory explosion of confetti, fireworks, and royal fanfare banner.
   - **Playful "No" Button**: Playfully runs away, dodges cursor/finger taps, and cycles funny sweet reasons why she can only say YES!
3. **📜 Step 3: Soulful Love Quotes & Secret Love Note**
   - Handpicked romantic love quotes written from the soul for Hafiza.
   - Interactive sealed envelope with wax heart seal that taps open to reveal a private love letter.
4. **🌟 Step 4: 6 Reasons Why Hafiza is My Princess**
   - Interactive royal virtues celebrating her radiant smile, mesmerizing eyes, pure kindness, laugh, strength, and uniqueness.
5. **🎓 Step 5: Official Princess Coronation Certificate**
   - Certified Proclamation declaring Princess Hafiza the ruler of your heart, with interactive "Tap to Crown" and printable certificate.

---

## 🔲 How to Generate & Share the QR Code

1. Open the website at **`http://localhost:3000`** in your browser.
2. Click **"Get Scannable QR Gift Card"** in the top bar or hero banner.
3. You will see the **Royal Princess QR Gift Card**:
   - High-contrast, scannable QR Code that leads directly to Hafiza's surprise.
   - Click **"Save QR Gift Card as Image (PNG)"** to download it as an image. You can send this image to Hafiza on WhatsApp, Instagram, or print it out on paper / greeting card!
   - When she points her phone camera at the QR code, the magical experience opens on her screen!
